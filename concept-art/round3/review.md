## Common problems
1. **Moss and secondary greenery come out photographic.** The model draws the hero plants in ink but leaves moss looking photographic, which breaks criterion 1 in most frames. Next round should name moss explicitly as a drawn element in every shot, or accept photo moss as a deliberate rule.
2. **HUD areas are busy.** Gravel and soil detail fill the bottom 15%, and foliage fills the top corners.
3. **Creatures are too big.** Woodlice and shrimp are drawn oversized for a jar view, and some still have smiling cartoon faces even though the prompt forbids them.
4. **Sparkle and bokeh.** Golden glitter and bokeh orbs creep in, especially in golden-hour shots.
5. **Light direction.** The model often keeps the round-2 side light from the references instead of light from behind the jar.
6. **Strengths.** The real-world rendering passes everywhere (criterion 2), and palette and line weight stay consistent across the set (criterion 8 mostly passes). The drawn-on-photo look is clearly established.
