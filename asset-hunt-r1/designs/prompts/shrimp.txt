SUBJECT: cherry shrimp, Neocaridina davidi (red cherry colour form), adult female about 2.5 cm long.
ANATOMY (must be correct):
- Body: a smooth carapace (cephalothorax) with a short, pointed, toothed rostrum projecting forward between the eyes; behind it a curved abdomen of 6 segments with a gentle hump at the third segment, bending down toward the tail.
- TAIL FAN: a central pointed telson flanked by ONE PAIR of paddle-shaped uropods on each side (total of 5 blades: telson plus 2 uropods left and 2 right, each uropod split into an inner and outer blade). Not three leaf blades.
- Head: two small black stalked eyes; ONE pair of long thin antennae (longer than the body, sweeping back) plus a pair of short forked antennules at the front; small flat antennal scales.
- Legs: 5 pairs of thin walking legs (pereiopods) under the carapace; the first two pairs are tiny and end in very small fan-tipped claws (chelae) used for picking food, NOT large crayfish pincers. 5 pairs of small feathery swimmerets (pleopods) under the abdomen.
- Colour: translucent cherry red with denser red patches, partly see-through body showing a slightly lighter red interior; slender and delicate.
- No face, no smile.
