SUBJECT: ramshorn snail, Planorbella duryi (family Planorbidae), adult, shell about 2 cm across.
ANATOMY (must be correct):
- The shell is PLANISPIRAL: coiled flat in ONE plane like a rolled ribbon or a cinnamon roll / coiled rope, NOT a raised cone or spire like a garden snail. In the side view you look at the flat face of the coil: a disc showing about 4 whorls that grow steadily wider toward the aperture, with the coil centre slightly sunken (umbilicate) on both sides. The shell is much taller than it is thick; seen edge-on it is a narrow rounded disc.
- Shell colour: translucent red-brown to reddish-amber, with faint fine growth lines across the whorls.
- The animal is carried with the shell upright, the disc standing vertical above the foot, aperture at the front-lower side.
- Soft body: dark red-brown, slightly translucent, a broad flat crawling foot underneath, a short rounded head.
- Exactly TWO long, thin, thread-like tentacles (no second shorter pair, no stalked eyes); the small dark eyes sit at the BASE of the tentacles on the inner side, NOT on the tips.
- No face, no mouth drawn as a smile.
