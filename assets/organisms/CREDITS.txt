developed with Senckenberg Museum für Naturkunde Görlitz / museum4punkt0

Public AR source: https://vr-bodenleben.senckenberg.de/tiere/laubstreu/landassel/
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes by ECOSPHERE: reduced original geometry from57,360 to5,354triangles;
removed photographic maps; matte sage/grey materials; added20-bone rig and
idle/walk/eat clips using the original seven leg pairs, antennae and jaws.
The source is static. Added gait is an artistic approximation, not museum
validation of motion. Juveniles/needs affect local material appearance.
Original creators do not endorse ECOSPHERE. No source anatomy was replaced
with primitive organisms. See ASSETS.md and scripts/adapt_woodlouse.py.

Ant worker, queen and naked pupa

Adaptations: matte charcoal palette, lowered stance, normalized direction,

Ant larva
