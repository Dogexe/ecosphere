# ECOSPHERE Scale Bible

Strategy: **coherent biological proportions, independent interaction targets**. One world unit is a composition unit, not a metre. Use close cameras and independent hit targets; never enlarge organisms for readability or tapping. Relative envelopes are deliberate stylization, not real-world scale calibration. Trees establish the upper layer, ferns the understory, clover the ground layer, moss the carpet. All dimensions below exclude picking volumes.

`src/scene/scale.ts` owns presentation targets and normalization. The ecological solver owns navigation. Changing artwork must never change resource stocks, feeding distances or saved positions. Stable individual variation is ±6%; biomass/development or actual woodlouse body mass controls growth. It never consumes the simulation RNG. A mature target describes full biomass, not every planted individual.

The pond solver now reserves conservative adult body clearances of0.24 for fish and0.07 for water fleas inside the existing0.93 water radius. These are independent physics parameters, not quantities derived from meshes or tap proxies. Imported adult fish bounds at maximum identity variation are checked over real animation/headings; the existing fallback is centered around its body/tail envelope. Placement, swimming and offspring use these limits. Legacy center-only shore saves still load, then surviving animals recover inward on the next step; wetland trajectories may deliberately differ. No fish was shrunk to hide bank clipping, and capture range remains0.18.

Plant observation reserves space for the inspector on side-panel layouts: transient12%projection shift with64%usable horizontal framing; actual OrbitControls permit the required focused distance beyond the ordinary21limit in very tall viewports. This changes projection, never meshes or organism positions. Phone stacked layouts stay centered. Prior projection is restored on return or subsurface entry.

Before the original scale pass, a full fern could spread ~2 units, all plants shared a generic vigor multiplier, springtails approached woodlouse size, and juvenile scale was missing or jumped at adulthood. Current targets normalize measured authored/imported bounds, separate groundcover from canopy, and keep fauna/props subordinate. Imported rig bounds must be measured after updating matrices/bones; their wrapper normalization sits above animated nodes. Pond/island simulation footprints remain unchanged after the recessed terrain pass. Shallow underwater exploration now uses a constrained below-surface camera; 3D aquatic movement and deep-water habitats remain future work. Imported lily pads keep their surface anchor at every growth scale; reed bases now meet the existing bed.
