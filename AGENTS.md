## Permanent policy: asset-first, modification-first

Search and inspect existing assets before creating or replacing significant organism/environment models. Prefer adapting a good asset into an optimized local GLB; verify anatomy, style, animations, cost and exact permissions, record provenance/modifications in `ASSETS.md`, then inspect it in the actual game. Custom geometry requires a recorded reason that available assets are unsuitable, unavailable or inferior. Never silently substitute primitives when retrieval fails. Preserve good models. Follow the Scale Bible; touch targets must not enlarge meshes or alter ecology. The current master directive authorizes autonomous selection/integration, superseding the earlier preview-only hold. This remains a personal/non-commercial game; record commercial/modification/distribution terms accurately without excluding compatible NC assets.
