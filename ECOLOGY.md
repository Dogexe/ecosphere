# Ecological grounding and model boundaries

## One seed-harvester colony (v3)

This is a generic functional guild, not a calibrated reconstruction of a named ant species. [Tschinkel & Kwapich2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5125654/) supports seed storage and larval processing in harvester ants, including strong seed-size/germination constraints. We abstract edible reproductive material within mature clover/grass/bloom biomass above70%capacity; we do not model seed size, husking, germination, trophallaxis or insect-protein requirements. Spores, leaves of immature plants and aquatic producers are unsuitable.

Workers move real surface distances and connected graph edges, harvest≤0.012material, carry to stores and transfer meals/nurse loads only at the appropriate chamber. A meal's digestion partitions55%metabolic outflow/35%organic/10%mineral return; larval provisioning retains75%, returns15%organic/5%mineral and loses5%metabolically. Eggs cost0.005queen gut:0.004egg/0.00075organic/0.00025metabolic. Brood maintenance, pantry spoilage and deaths have explicit destinations. `antMaterialError` checks imported+harvested against current body/gut/carry/brood/stores plus outflows. This local material-equivalent ledger does not establish global elemental conservation; existing producer carbon uptake and animal energy proxies remain uncalibrated.

Larvae grow from transported food, then pupate; emerged workers keep fixed body mass and spend24model hours as pale tenerals. [Social administration of juvenile hormone to larvae increases body size and nutritional needs for pupation(2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10731321/) supports separating larval nutrition/development from adult body size, not our numerical rates. Egg12h/larval≥24h plus funded≥0.039mass/pupa24h are deliberately compressed game parameters, not observed development times. Brood clocks require suitable moisture; only actual nurse visits transfer larval food. A lone surviving worker can alternate care with foraging rather than being prohibited from nursing.

Hydration represents body water at hydration/100×body×0.4. Soil rehydration and emergence withdraw that amount; death returns remaining body water (capacity-capped), while maintenance loss is untracked vapor outflow. Initial body water arrives with explicitly imported adults. Energy is a need proxy paid by digestion and travel, not calories. Nested temperature is a buffered forcing approximation, not a heat/3Dwater solver. Workers now excavate connected refuge volumes when crowded, supplied and in suitable soil; reserve funds work and a separate geometric soil-volume proxy records aggregated entrance spoil. No calibrated soil mechanics, mating flights, genetics, pheromone chemistry, ant predation, disease or inter-colony conflict. Stress tests can and do lose colonies; no self-balancing or scripted recovery is applied.

Spatial resource handling uses the nearest of the existing four surface soil columns at each actual chamber/actor position. Nursery moisture sets brood development and funds emerged-worker body water; nursing waste/brood upkeep return there. Egg costs return at the queen room, spoilage at the granary, and excavation samples the actual advancing face once per fixed hour. A wet entrance cannot hydrate a nursery in a dry column for free. This is a coarse spatial allocation rule, not underground water diffusion, flooding, a measured depth gradient or new moisture stocks. Chamber tending returns to that same real surface pocket; UI actions do not advance time or grant water until an explicit input.

Recorded tiny ant remains use the same moisture/fungus-dependent fractional decay as before. The final numerical cleanup is capped at 2.5% of the initial pool (and at most0.001 units), avoiding a whole tiny body being transferred at once because the historical absolute cutoff was larger than the body. The final trace is still transferred to the same soil pool; this threshold is a computational approximation, not a measured decomposition rate. Legacy untagged/non-ant pools retain their prior cutoff.

Need priorities use actual reserves/stores/carried material. Adult hunger or larval demand can interrupt night rest, while heat and teneral protection remain. Eggs/pupae cannot receive nursery meals; committed carers finish deliveries unless queen reserve is critical. Starving carriers can ingest their own harvested load. These are explicit lightweight behavioral approximations. Recorded ant death provenance supplies real form/body/count/depth to rendering; decomposition still transfers the same pool locally, outside the already closed colony outflow. Fallen orientation and shrinkage show remaining material without claiming skeletal decay mechanics. Cohort inspection reads actual developmental clocks/funded mass; transient birth-cohort events support observation without introducing saved genetics or a new material pool.

New food-loading care trips require an actual meal in stores; an unloaded adult is not recalled into an empty granary. A ready adult carrying real harvested material may instead provision a needy queen/larva in its actual chamber when stores are empty. This changes routing, not diet, load capacity, material partition or metabolic rates. It is a generic task-policy approximation, not an empirically calibrated role reversal: [harvester-ant activation experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3767282/) demonstrate regulation by interactions and returning foragers; [Gordon's life-history review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11528356/) specifically describes one-way progression to foraging in P.barbatus. The game has neither that species-specific task history nor its interaction-mediated activation mechanism. The temporary “nursing” activity denotes delivered provisioning, not a claim that mature P.barbatus foragers become permanent brood workers.

The queen may also pay the existing egg cost from her own gut when there is no brood, her reserve is at least70, her gut contains at least0.015, three adults are ready and at least0.006 food is actually stored or carried. These are game thresholds, not empirical harvester-ant physiology. Food being carried is not reserved or deducted by egg-laying: later larval growth still needs real deliveries and may fail. This permits replacement generations when food moves through the nest without accumulating, but reproduction competes with adult survival; matched tended experiments sometimes lose the queen sooner. The model currently has synchronized founding-worker ages and does not guarantee a viable multigeneration population.

An unloaded worker already at the granary can pause to digest its paid meal below the existing35reserve care-ready threshold. Existing self-feeding and carried deliveries precede this decision; an empty gut or recovered reserve releases it to other work. Rest uses the existing lower maintenance cost, not a reserve grant, and digestion retains its material/waste partition. This generic need-based policy prevents repeated fed-but-depleted departure/return trips; it is not a calibrated resting physiology. In twelve matched2400h cases two tended queens survived the horizon, others died (one tended queen83h sooner), and foodless controls still failed. The reserve/food/role tradeoff remains a game-model balance limitation.

Woodlouse contact is a narrow overdamped positional constraint, independent
from rendered meshes and touch targets. After feeding/death/reproduction,
surviving and newborn woodlice receive at most0.08world-unit correction per
model hour on existing land navigation. A0.16adult soft radius scales with
cbrt(funded body mass); an actively courting pair can remain0.28apart, within
the existing0.3pairing range. These are uncalibrated game parameters, not
measured biomechanics or oriented body hulls. Contact neither grants nor
spends metabolic reserve/material; kinetic energy and physical work are not
modeled. All original digestion/brood/death transfers still run before this
pass. Other species' body contact remains simplified. Old v1/v2 contents
remain loadable, but future paths can change after this correction. Earlier
trajectory hashes remain historical; current twelve240-step hashes are in
`tests/fixtures/contact-trajectories.json`.

ECOSPHERE models functional ecological roles with compressed, uncalibrated game rates. It is not an environmental forecast. The rendering reads organism/resource state; decorative grass and terrain dressing do not count as extra species.

## Detritivores

Primary research supports terrestrial isopods consuming leaf litter, affecting its breakdown and nutrient release, and showing reduced feeding/survival under dry conditions. The game's woodlouse group follows these directions; it does not reproduce any measured rate or a named species' complete diet.

- Des Marteaux et al. (2020), leaf-litter microcosms with two isopod species: [Microbial Ecology, DOI 10.1007/s00248-020-01527-4](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32440700/). Effects on fungi differed by species; ECOSPHERE therefore does not apply a universal fictional fungal-growth bonus.
- Yang, Shao & Li (2020), field microcosms and nutrient release: [Geoderma 114546](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016706120300586).
- Leclercq-Dransart et al. (2019), four isopod species under drier conditions: [European Journal of Soil Biology, DOI 10.1016/j.ejsobi.2018.11.005](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1164556318302231).

The game routes consumed litter into 40% fecal organic stock, 35% available nutrient stock, and 25% untracked respiratory loss. Woodlouse offspring cost 0.22 body-material units from litter and split the parent's energy reserve. These values are design parameters. Body material is fixed per represented animal; reserve energy has its own scale. Reserve-threshold reproduction is a coarse population surrogate: pairing and sex are not simulated. Leaf chemistry, microbial species, age structure, growth allometry, and actual carbon/nitrogen stoichiometry are not resolved.

## Predator increment grounding

The beetle is a carnivorous functional archetype. Real carabid diets are diverse; this model does not represent every ground beetle or calibrated capture rates. Primary woodland gut-content work detected multiple invertebrate prey groups, including woodlice and herbivorous Lepidoptera: [Šerić Jelaska et al. (2014), DOI 10.14411/eje.2014.079](https://www.eje.cz/pdfs/eje/2014/05/06.pdf).

Predators can also change plant damage through prey behavior, with effects varying among plant groups. A terrestrial grasshopper–spider experiment supports this mechanism, not a universal benefit or a beetle-specific rate: [Beckerman, Uriarte & Schmitz (1997), experimental behavior-mediated trophic cascade](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC23467/). ECOSPHERE tests its own comparative outcomes instead of applying a scripted plant bonus.

Hunters perceive prey within 2.6 scene units, pursue when reserve is at most 66%, and catch at 0.28 units. Nearby grazers/woodlice flee instead of feeding. A catch partitions fixed prey body material (grazer 0.42, woodlouse 0.22): 20% of material available after any offspring body is returned to local litter; 80% enters an untracked metabolic/assimilation pool. This pool supplies reserve energy at a game conversion factor of 60; reserve units are not additional material units. A well-reserved hunter with abundant prey can allocate 0.34 units directly from a sufficiently large catch to a newborn body, then split its reserve 58%/42%. It cannot fund a beetle body from a single woodlouse. Beetle energy death returns its 0.34-unit body stock. These are explicit game design coefficients, not measured carabid estimates; no full carbon ledger is claimed.

## Existing simplified cycles

- Fungal processing redistributes local litter into nutrients (72%), fungal biomass (18%), and respiratory loss (10%); fungal maintenance returns 60% to litter and loses the rest. Isolated detrital stocks have non-creation tests.
- Producers depend on local water/nutrients and species-specific light/moisture preferences. Growth represents photosynthetic input from an untracked atmosphere. Decline/death returns a modeled litter fraction; recruitment transfers parent biomass directly.
- Grazers consume living biomass, spend reserve energy, fund offspring with plant material, and return a fixed body stock on death. Introduced organisms and materials are explicit external inputs; removed organisms are exports.
- Four equal-area soil pockets are coarse compartments. Water evaporates; transport/runoff, atmospheric carbon/oxygen, and a complete common-unit material ledger are still absent. Global soil values are patch means, not extra duplicate stocks.

## Habitat starters (M11)

Mixed garden preserves the original initial state. Shaded floor and sunny meadow change only initial stocks, light, and resident proportions; no habitat identity enters simulation or saves. These are terrestrial tray experiments, not complete biomes. The shared decorative pond does not simulate an aquatic food web.

For a paired environmental check, four identical producers (2 units biomass / 8 capacity each, no consumers/fungi, nutrients 100) ran 40 steps. At initial water 80/light .42 the final fern/moss gains were 2.97/1.99 units, versus bloom/clover 0.13/0.02. At water 50/light .92 bloom/clover gained 3.49/2.71, fern gained .08, and moss died. This checks the existing stylized niche rules, not measured species performance.

An additional 240-step starter comparison used seeds 17/183/2217, added water whenever mean water fell below 45, and added no litter. Shaded floor ended with 46.40–61.77 producer units, 12.94–13.03 total fungal units, and 14.54–19.05 grazed units. Sunny meadow ended with 89.13–92.67 producer units, 1.88–1.90 fungal units, and 64.13–68.67 grazed units. These starters differ in community and initial resources as well as light; they are not a controlled estimate of light alone. Both still need tending, and outcomes can diverge with different interventions.

Plant inspection reports the shared growth-minus-turnover equation at the current patch, before consumer damage, recruitment, neighbor uptake, and the next fungal flux. It identifies the lowest normalized moisture/light/nutrient response. It is a local outlook, not a promise of next-step biomass or a calibrated diagnosis.

## Composition-based inputs (M12)

One transfer path accepts 0–20 units of each existing stock per targeted equal-area pocket. Water/organic/available nutrients are independent relative model quantities, not fractions that sum to a measured substance mass. Whole-tray inputs target all four pockets; receipts report totals, while UI world summaries remain means. Water retained up to the 100-unit local capacity plus explicit outflow equals requested water. Organic/nutrient imports match their stock changes exactly. The existing water and compost controls still import 18 water / 16 organic units per pocket through this path.

Input addition does not change organisms, time, or random state. Fungi/woodlice must consume organic substrate through existing rules; mineral nutrients enter the already root-accessible pool and do not feed decomposers. Tests isolate both mechanisms and verify deterministic continuation. Receipt outflow is not hydrological transport: it does not run into adjacent cells or a simulated pond.

The composer is a simplified experiment with three pools, not a validated representation of semen, waste, fertilizer chemistry, salts, pH, pathogens, or oxygen demand. Named biological substances and richer composition require those interactions and appropriate parameters before making specific claims.
# Current extensions — 2026-10-25

## Ecological photoperiod — 2026-10-25

The optional v2 climate flag enables a shared24-hour clock. New UI worlds begin at08:00; old/missing flags remain constant. Light rises from06:00 to noon and reaches zero at18:00. The existing temperature setting is a baseline, with a bounded±3°C sinusoid peaking at15:00; the derived value is never saved back into the baseline. This is a fixed12-hour day and imposed thermal forcing, not a latitude/season/weather/heat-capacity model.

Producer growth coefficients were set for continuous daily-average game potential. In cycle mode, the positive sine light curve is normalized to a24-step integral of24 (peak≈3.16), concentrating that potential into daylight without adding a daily resource grant. Species preference uses peak local canopy light; instantaneous photons gate photosynthesis. Gross growth pays0.12water/0.65nutrient units, bounded by available stocks and remaining plant capacity plus turnover. Turnover returns42% to organic stock, with the rest an open model loss. Night has zero photosynthetic input, ongoing turnover and developmental progress. Aquatic photosynthesis supplies modeled oxygen; aquatic plant respiratory loss, algal respiration, animals, decay and atmospheric exchange continue at night. Oxygen can still rise at night from air exchange; there is no hardcoded nightly depletion.

Real plants can use stored carbohydrates for nighttime metabolism and structural growth, as demonstrated by [Scialdone et al.'s starch-budget experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3691572/). ECOSPHERE currently lumps structure/reserve into one producer biomass pool rather than modeling starch separately; it does not claim that real plants cannot grow at night. Day/night oxygen consequences are motivated by [measured freshwater diel community activity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7261829/), not calibrated dissolved-oxygen predictions. No common-unit atmospheric carbon ledger or CAM physiology is implemented.

Selected functional groups have activity windows: grazer/tiger-beetle/fish are diurnal, woodlouse/springtail nocturnal, water fleas continually active. Woodlouse rhythms/sheltering are supported by [field observations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11822361/) and the [light/hydration interactions discussed by Cloudsley-Thompson](https://symposium.cshlp.org/content/25/345.full.pdf). The visible tiger-beetle guild uses a diurnal strategy compatible with [UCR observations](https://entmuseum.ucr.edu/bug-spotlight); other real tiger-beetle species can be nocturnal. Grazer/springtail/fish schedules and all numeric thresholds are design approximations, not species-wide physiological claims. No endogenous circadian oscillator or vertical plankton migration is modeled.

Scheduled rest halves positive maintenance expenditure (consistent with the existing fed-woodlouse rest reduction); it never grants reserve. Water stress, hydration, digestion, aging and funded broods continue. Severe hunger (<25reserve), woodlouse hydration<35 and immediate danger interrupt rest. Prey assess a hunter after its upcoming basal payment, so a hunter crossing the hunger threshold cannot secretly wake/catch sleeping prey. Resting fish also reduce positive respiratory cost; hypoxia/drying penalties remain. Digested substrate can replenish reserve during rest because the material was already ingested. Mating/reproduction still require material/reserve and suitable contact/refuge, rather than being triggered by a clock phase.

This section supersedes older milestone descriptions of a decorative pond and animals without age. The following are simplified, deterministic game models, not calibrated ecological predictions.

- **Canopy and niches:** tree biomass/distance controls local light attenuation and evaporation. Grass is a sun-loving grazer resource; springtails graze fungal biomass with a moisture-dependent, finite food target. A bite partitions 25% to litter, 30% to nutrients and 45% to open metabolism. Body/offspring material comes from fungi. These choices are motivated by [fungal-grazing experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16175795/) and [canopy/understory research](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2020.01051/full); the numerical fractions/ranges are design parameters.
- **Pond:** water, organic matter, mineral nutrients, algae and oxygen are separate relative stocks. Algae and aquatic plants use mineral nutrients and light; water fleas graze algae; fish capture fleas. A saturating flea feeding response prevents unlimited food intake. Fish catches partition prey body into litter, capped stored material and open metabolism; reproduction must fund a new body from stored material and split existing reserve. Organic decomposition is oxygen-limited, capped by available oxygen/2.5; it cannot process a full aerobic flux without paying its oxygen cost. Air exchange is an external source/sink, not a closed atmospheric cycle. This is consistent in principle with [experimental trophic cascades](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4237340/) and [Daphnia hypoxia research](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4441/14/20/3213). Oxygen is **not mg/L**, and no real species-specific toxicity forecast is claimed.
- **Climate:** rain explicitly imports water into four soil pockets and the pond, recording retained/outflow totals. Temperature changes evaporation/metabolism and plant thermal fit. It is not seasonal weather, a fluid solver, or a calibrated Q10 model. Pond water additions stop at capacity; excess is outside the modeled pond, not transferred into soil.
- **Plant history:** propagules import 0.55 biomass; moisture/temperature drive developmental progress. Photosynthetic growth begins after germination, still limited by the shared nutrient/light/water response. Recruits transfer parent biomass; age-dependent senescence precedes mortality. Fern/moss propagules are displayed as spores; the model does not reproduce their complete alternation of generations. Tree crowns and all model sizes follow `SCALE_BIBLE.md`; geometry never changes ecological rates.
- **Representative animal history — woodlouse:** body mass 0.22 adult / 0.055 newborn. Ingestion removes litter into a gut (capacity 0.13). Up to 0.04 per step is digested, yielding 40% fecal litter, 35% nutrients, up to 15% growing body material and the remaining fraction as untracked metabolic loss. Growth also costs reserve energy. Energy units are an activity proxy, not extra material. Hydration absorption removes water from local soil; evaporation/body water is not a closed hydrological ledger. Maturity requires age >=60 and body mass >=0.20. Both sexes require >=45 reserve to seek a mate; four close courtship steps cost each parent 8 reserve. Conception transfers up to 0.11 local litter material into a brood, followed by 24 model hours of brooding. Offspring are released only in a moist litter refuge with capacity, split existing maternal reserve, and receive transferred brood mass/hydration. This direct substrate-to-brood bookkeeping is a **coarse resource-cost surrogate**, not literal reproductive physiology. Parent/offspring identity is tracked; genetics, inbreeding effects, molting, sperm storage and disease are absent. The current mate rule excludes parent/child and same-mother pairings, which can constrain a very small closed population.
- **Biological basis and time limits:** maternal marsupial brooding and released juveniles resembling adults are grounded in [Porcellio embryonic observations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3335405/) and [experimental brooding/juvenile-growth work](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4525036/). The 24-hour gestation, offspring count, lifetime, gut partitions and energy thresholds are compressed game parameters, **not measured durations or budgets**. Other five animal roles have age/juvenile scale and resource-funded reproduction but do not yet have a complete sex/digestion/mating/metamorphosis model. No biologically complete caterpillar claim is made for the segmented grazer.
- **Death and decomposition:** actual woodlouse body+gut+brood, aquatic body+stored material, and other animal fixed bodies enter local remains. Producer death contributes its established 42% litter fraction; the rest remains an open model loss. Moisture/temperature/fungi affect gradual release from remains into local organic stock, with no double death/capture deposit. Dry remains persist. The bounded visual record aggregates only within a compartment, preserving material. IDs remain monotonic across extinctions to avoid confusing living descendants with reused parent IDs.

New tests cover ingestion/digestion, pairing/gestation/costs, a natural forest-born juvenile growing into an adult, flee-time maturation/gestation, energy caps, local decay and v1/v2 continuation. A habitat starter is an initial condition, never a scripted birth/capture schedule. Full biomes, common-unit carbon/nutrient accounting, calibrated chemistry, aquatic seeding after extinction, named biological fluids and physical-device validation remain future work.

This stage/presentation correction does not change the post-correction ecological trajectories.
